The earlier benchmark showed that provenance-backed analysis improves semantic chain recovery, but it offered limited family coverage, did not isolate note-like hard-benign pressure clearly, and lacked explicit benign/malicious lookalike pairs that hold relay shape and action surface constant while changing the security semantics. benchmark\_v2 addresses these gaps by expanding along risk-mechanism families rather than by random case inflation. The resulting benchmark is aligned with semantic chain recovery and auditable root-cause attribution: it asks not only whether a detector fires, but whether the recovered explanation can distinguish sensitive-source outward transfer from public-content mirror, approved upload, or local-only note/report generation.
